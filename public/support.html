Support

HyperMixer and HyperJudge

Get help

Report a problem or ask a question on GitHub Issues. Include your device, the app, and what you tapped before the problem appeared.

HyperMixer

How do I save a finished video? Tap Save or share under the video and choose Save Video or the app you want to post to.

Where is my data? On your device, and in your own private sync space if you sign in. See the privacy policy.

How do I delete my account? Open Connect, then tap Delete account.

HyperJudge

How do I play? Open HyperJudge, go to Setup and do the three steps: add the shortcut, put it on AssistiveTouch (or Back Tap), and open a reel. Tap the button on any reel, pick a guess, and your score shows up as a banner.

My points aren't on the leaderboard. Sign in, copy your leaderboard code from Setup, and paste it when the shortcut asks. If you added the shortcut before, replace it.

How do I delete my account? Tap your name at the top, then Delete account.
