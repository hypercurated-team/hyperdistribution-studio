Privacy Policy

HyperMixer and HyperJudge · Last updated 26 November 2026

This policy covers HyperMixer (web, iOS and Android) and HyperJudge (iOS), both made by the HyperMixer team at Bright Solutions. We collect as little as we can, we never sell data, and we never use it for ads.

Your account (optional)

You can use both apps without an account. If you sign in, your Hypercurated account is handled by Google Firebase Authentication. We keep your email address, your name if the sign-in provider shares it (Apple, Google or Microsoft), and an account ID.

HyperMixer

Your studio. Brands, brand brains, posts, plans and settings are stored on your device. If you sign in, they are also synced to your own private space in Google Firebase so you can use them on other devices. Only your account can read or write that space.

Photos and videos. The app reads only the photos and videos you allow. It understands them on your device (for example, finding faces, blurry shots and duplicates) and does not upload your library. When you use Auto-pick or attach an image to an AI request, small previews of the chosen images are sent with that request to Anthropic (see AI requests). Videos you make are saved on your device.

AI requests. When you use an AI feature, the text of that request, and any image previews attached to it, go through our server to Anthropic, which returns the answer. We do not store the request or the answer. We keep a daily count of AI requests per account or device to enforce the free allowance. If you enter your own Claude API key, it is sent with your requests and never stored on our server.

Reading links. When you ask HyperMixer to learn from a web page, our server fetches that public page and returns its text. We do not keep a copy.

Cloud files (optional). If you connect Google Drive, OneDrive or Dropbox, the sign-in token is encrypted and kept on your device. Our server uses it only to get a short-lived access token. Files are downloaded by your device straight from the provider, never through our server.

HeyGen (optional). If you add a HeyGen key and ask for an avatar video, the script, avatar and key go through our server to HeyGen. We do not store them.

HyperJudge

A round. When you start a round, the HyperJudge shortcut reads the text on your screen on your iPhone. It sends that text (not the screenshot), your guess and your leaderboard code to our server. The server works out which public creator is showing and looks up their public follower count, then returns your score.

What we keep. Your leaderboard name, your leaderboard code, your points and your current streak. Public follower counts are cached for one day. If you play without an account, your points are kept under the name the shortcut sends.

What we don't do

We do not sell data, show ads, use tracking or analytics SDKs, or build profiles. Neither app tracks you across other companies' apps or websites.

Service providers

Vercel hosts our server and keeps standard technical logs (such as IP address and time of request) for security for a limited period. Google Firebase handles sign-in and HyperMixer sync. Upstash stores HyperJudge scores. Anthropic answers AI requests.

Deleting your data

In HyperMixer, open Connect and tap Delete account. This deletes your account and everything synced to it. In HyperJudge, tap your name, then Delete account. This deletes your account, leaderboard name, code and scores. Deleting the app removes what is stored on your device. You can also ask us to delete your data through support.

Children

Neither app is directed at children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. Questions: support.
