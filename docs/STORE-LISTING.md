## Description

HyperMixer is the content engine for businesses. Founders, marketing teams and agencies use it to keep every brand posting on every channel, without a content team's budget.

Teach it your brand once. Paste your website and profiles, and HyperMixer writes a brand brain: voice, audience, pillars and hooks. Every post after that sounds like you.

Swipe through ready-made posts. HyperMixer drafts ideas for each brand and channel. Swipe right to approve, left to skip.

Edit videos from your own clips. Pick photos and clips from your library, or from Google Drive, OneDrive or Dropbox through the Files app. HyperMixer understands them on your phone, picks the best shots and cuts a short-form video. Save it to your camera roll or share it.

Line it up and ship it. Every approved post gets a time, a caption and its video, ready to share to the app you post from.

See who is behind. The dashboard shows each brand's posts against its weekly target, so nothing goes quiet.

- Brand brains from your links
- Swipe to approve AI-drafted posts
- Short-form video edits from your own clips, read on your phone
- Publish queue with captions and videos
- Weekly targets per brand and channel
- Works for one brand or a portfolio of brands
- Optional account to sync across devices

## App privacy (App Store Connect)

Tracking: **No**. Data collected, all **linked to the user**, used only for **App Functionality**, not used for tracking:

- Contact info: Email address, Name
- Identifiers: User ID
- User content: Other user content (brands and posts synced when signed in)

Not collected: location, contacts, health, financial info, browsing or search history, usage data, diagnostics, photos or videos (previews sent with an AI request are not stored).

## Review notes (App Store)

HyperMixer is a content planning and video editing tool for businesses. Signing in is optional: tap "Not now" on the first screen to use the app without an account. AI features work without setup (built in, with a daily free allowance). To try video editing, open Edit, pick a post, tap Add media and choose a few photos or clips. Photos are analysed on the device. Account deletion: Connect > Delete account.
